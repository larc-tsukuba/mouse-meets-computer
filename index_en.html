Laboratory Animal Resource Center, University of Tsukuba is working on developing software to support research using genetically modified animals.

Research Overview

Generating genetically modified animals involves many stages, from target gene selection to genotyping.
We develop tools that support hypothesis generation and experimentation at each stage, pioneering the next generation of laboratory animal science.
